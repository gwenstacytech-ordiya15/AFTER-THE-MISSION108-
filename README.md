# AFTER-THE-MISSION 🚀
AFTER THE MISSION is an interactive, gamified digital museum helping students explore what happens to real NASA spacecraft after their missions end.
**NASA Space Apps Challenge | Challenge: Abandoned but Not Forgotten**  
🌐 **Live Interactive Website:** [https://github.io](https://github.io)

---

### 🛰️ Project Overview
**AFTER THE MISSION** is a gamified, student-friendly digital museum designed to teach young learners what happens to real NASA robotic explorers once their missions end—and why their hardware remains on the Moon, Mars, Saturn, and interstellar space.

### ✨ Key Features
* **Interactive 3D Shoebox Diorama Map:** Clickable hotspots overlaid on our custom-built 4-zone physical space diorama (*Moon, Mars, Saturn, Deep Space*).
* **Gamified NASA Archivist XP System:** Students earn **+25 XP** per spacecraft dossier inspected and **+100 XP** for completing the Space Detective Quiz.
* **Printable Junior Archivist Certificate:** Unlocks an official classroom certificate students can personalize and save as a PDF.
* **Classroom DIY Shoebox Guide:** Step-by-step instructions and real progress photos enabling teachers and students to recreate the physical 3D model in 60 minutes using recycled supplies.
* **Young Explorers Showcase Gallery:** Allows students across classrooms to submit photos of their own 3D space models.
